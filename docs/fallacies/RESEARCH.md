## Search themes

- Politics: climate, migration, surveillance, asylum procedure, public appointments, armed exports, assisted suicide, coalition decisions.
- Everyday speech: tidying a room, weather preferences, private time, lateness, meetings, prices and quality, school performance, sports/hobby sayings.
- Argument mechanics: shifted proof criteria, selective studies, expert selection that presupposes its conclusion, repetition, majority claims, naturalness, invalid reverse implications, sunk costs, source credibility.
- Legitimate lookalikes: clarifying a position, checking source independence, practical consequence warnings, help requests, consistent standards, and risk assessment.

## What “deep” means here

- Ran multiple targeted discovery searches, then retrieved actual pages rather than labeling search snippets.
- Followed links to specialized articles and references, not just landing pages.
- Read four Seniorentreff thread pages; two Allmystery pages; original ICE-Treff complaint, quoted response, clarification request, and substantive reply; the Netz-Treff parent and follow-ups.
- Checked parliamentary passages in the official PDF, in addition to their secondary analysis. Checked Bush's line in the official archived speech.
- Reviewed **85 distinct candidate URLs from 41 hosts**. Retrieved **73 distinct pages/documents** successfully. Used **59 new source records from 31 hosts**; rejected or retained the remainder as discovery/context only.
- Did not retry blocked hosts through alternate identities, gateways, or archive mirrors. Reddit was robots-disallowed; several other forum sites were inaccessible.

Not every search hit was useful. A nature-photography result titled “Totschlagargumente” was a photograph with a pun, not an argument. A forum definition repost had no concrete case. Some reference pages confused categories, supplied only definitions, or offered a rhetorical accusation without sufficient context. Those did not become positive training cases.

The additions contain **10 actual written exchanges, 8 forum-posted illustrative/remembered examples, 4 official-transcript-checked speech passages, 5 secondary-reported political remarks, 77 website lessons, and 2 historical illustrations**. Forum examples are not spoken-debate recordings. Most clear positives are educational examples; many authentic passages remain ambiguous or legitimate. That distinction is intentional, not missing annotation.

## Still missing

This is a substantially larger inspection library, **not a complete census of fallacies, a balanced benchmark, or proof of real-world classifier performance**.
