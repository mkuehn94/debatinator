## Verification and dataset limits

Dates, runtimes, and descriptions were retrieved through yt-dlp. German automatic captions were downloaded into ignored `data/recommendations/`. These are caption-based format checks, not listening or visual verification. Automatic captions cannot establish with certainty that no third voice ever speaks.

The recent shortlisted sources feature political streamers/callers, not two elected politicians. No recent elected-politician-versus-elected-politician recording satisfying the strict no-moderator condition was verified in this search.

The two recent candidates share a host, so they are useful starter material, not a representative benchmark. Add different speakers and political perspectives before making general quality claims.
